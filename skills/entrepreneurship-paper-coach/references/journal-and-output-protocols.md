# Journal and Output Protocols

## Current journal boundary snapshot

Verified 2026-08-17. Treat this as routing context, not permanent policy. Reopen the official page before any fit or submission-readiness conclusion.

### Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice (ETP)

- Official instructions: https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/etp
- Current scope includes original conceptual and empirical work that develops, tests, or extends theory relating to entrepreneurship broadly defined.
- The journal accepts multiple empirical paradigms and conceptual work; do not impose a quantitative template.
- Its 2026 anniversary editorial argues for work that reveals how entrepreneurship operates and exports insights rather than merely importing theory: https://doi.org/10.1177/10422587261441596. Label this `[ENT-FTE-2026]`, not policy.

### Journal of Business Venturing (JBV)

- Official aims and scope: https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-business-venturing
- Current mission emphasizes useful and interesting theories, narratives, and interpretations of the antecedents, mechanisms, and consequences of entrepreneurship across disciplines, functions, and contexts.
- Test phenomenon centrality and explanatory value; do not reduce fit to the presence of ventures or entrepreneurs in the sample.

### Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal (SEJ)

- Official aims and scope: https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/1932443x/homepage/productinformation.html
- Current scope welcomes theory-driven and phenomenon-driven work at micro, meso, and macro levels and includes quantitative, qualitative, and conceptual research.
- Require an explicit strategic-entrepreneurship payoff rather than assuming all entrepreneurship work fits.

### Academy of Management Journal (AMJ)

- Official submission page: https://www.aom.org/publications/journals/publishing-with-aom/author-and-reviewer-resources/author-resources/submitting-to-journal/
- Current mission requires substantial empirical research that advances management theory and informs practice for a broad management audience.
- Use `$amj-ae-coach` as the governing journal layer and this skill as the entrepreneurship overlay.

## Live recheck record

For a journal-fit or pre-submission assessment, record:

- target journal and article type;
- official pages opened;
- verification date;
- mission and article-type fit;
- length, format, anonymization, data, ethics, transparency, and AI requirements;
- special-issue instructions if applicable;
- unchecked items marked `[[CURRENT POLICY NOT VERIFIED]]`.

## Topic pressure-test output

Return:

1. Gate and verdict on significance, curiosity, entrepreneurial centrality, conversation change, leverage, scope, and relevance.
2. Focal entrepreneurship conversation and proposed change.
3. Strongest rival framing.
4. Theory input-transformation-output map.
5. Minimum evidence needed to distinguish the explanation.
6. One-sentence topic thesis with verification markers.
7. Go, redesign, narrow, or redirect-journal recommendation.

Use this thesis form only as a scaffold:

`By examining [entrepreneurial phenomenon] through [mechanism/evidence], the study revises [conversation] by showing [bounded advance] under [conditions].`

## Design pre-mortem output

| Threat | Claim affected | Why current design cannot resolve it | Repair before data/analysis | Residual risk | Permitted wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then identify irreversible decisions, validation needs, analyses that must be planned, and complementary evidence that would change the inference.

## Section-coach output

Before rewriting, provide:

1. the section's job and whether the draft performs it;
2. an argument or evidence map;
3. fatal/major/minor issues anchored to paragraphs or sentences;
4. cross-section dependencies;
5. proposed structural moves in order;
6. an explicit authorization request naming the exact rewrite scope.

## Authorized rewrite output

```markdown
### Candidate manuscript text
<authorized scope only>

### Change rationale
- [Wording] <clarity or flow>
- [Structure] <argument order>
- [Substantive—author approval required] <claim change>

### Items for author verification
- [[AUTHOR VERIFY: exact item]]

### AI-use log increment
| Stage | Model/tool | Purpose | Human verification required |
|---|---|---|---|
```

Do not silently strengthen novelty, causal language, generalizability, or effect interpretation.

## Full-manuscript red-team output

Return:

1. gate and scope/desk-screen rationale;
2. maximum defensible claim;
3. promise-delivery matrix from [reviewer-logic.md](reviewer-logic.md);
4. fatal, major, and minor issues;
5. separate revisions requiring theory, analysis/data, writing, or compliance;
6. single highest-leverage revision;
7. submission-readiness checklist after live policy verification.

## Novelty verification record

Record:

- claim checked;
- databases/search systems;
- query families and synonyms;
- dates and inclusion/exclusion boundaries;
- adjacent conversations and nearest studies;
- result: supported within scope, weakened, contradicted, or unresolved.

Prefer “Within the stated search boundary, no directly matching study was located as of [date]” over universal absence claims.
